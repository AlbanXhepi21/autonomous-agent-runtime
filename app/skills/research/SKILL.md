# Research

Use this skill to investigate factual questions and present appropriately qualified conclusions.

- Define the claim or decision and the evidence needed before searching.
- Prefer primary and authoritative sources; use secondary sources for context or comparison.
- Check publication dates and whether information is still current.
- Cross-check important claims and identify material conflicting evidence.
- Separate directly supported facts from inferences, and track uncertainty or gaps.
- Stop when the evidence is sufficient for the goal, explaining relevant limitations.
